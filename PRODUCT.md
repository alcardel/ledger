# Credit Appraisal Workbench
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Confirmed: Next.js/TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Celery/Redis, Ollama, WorkOS.
## Users
Indian retail and MSME relationship managers, analysts, credit approvers, policy managers, risk reviewers, organization administrators.
## Product Purpose
Source-linked financial evidence, configurable credit policy and Jev recommendations support auditable officer decisions for term loans and working-capital facilities.
## Operating Context
Desktop internal finance operations. Local Mac demo with synthetic evidence, English and INR. Scale via independent workers and shared infrastructure.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Administrators can override business gates, including their own cases, with mandatory reason and preserved history. Tenant boundaries cannot be overridden. Missing integrations are explicitly unavailable. No fabricated AI responses. Policy, evidence and assessment versions remain immutable.
## Brand Commitments
Financially rich, readable navy and light workbench. Clear Indian financial terminology; code-first UI.
## Evidence on Hand
Synthetic documents will be generated. WorkOS account exists but credentials are not in the workspace. Jev access is not configured.
## Product Principles
Evidence before judgment. Missing is not zero. Every exception has an owner and resolution. Financial calculations are reproducible. Overrides are explicit and auditable.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls, labeled forms, visible focus and non-color status indicators.

Loan scope: 12 initial configurable products, with administrator-created additions. Manual officer bureau entry is available now; Roopya is displayed as pending onboarding. Rates are illustrative. Monthly flat/reducing calculations are supported; specialized schedules are explicitly outside the current calculator.

Primary entry point: Credit Overview, always first in the sidebar and the default home screen. Approval Workbench is immediately below it; prioritize pending approvals, then cases in review. Keep repeated demonstration labels out of the operational UI; retain sample-data provenance in records and audit.
